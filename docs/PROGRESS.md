## Preserved scope

The [supported profile](SUPPORTED-PROFILE.md) stays delivered: CMYK/YCCK opaque,
Zopfli unavailable, crypto excluded. Other [compatibility](COMPATIBILITY.md)
requirements are not waived. Ranged argv/job reading, catalog/recovery policy,
stable leases/complete fallback, input funding/early closure, immutable publication
and multisource independence remain. Approximate accounting protects significant
capacity/growth overlap, amplification/decompression/work limits, cancellation,
cleanup, integrity and surviving aliases. Charges are not retained heap/RSS.
Exact CLR sizes, individual small-object/metadata-alias receipts, GC rounds and
bookkeeping-byte precision remain withdrawn; see [governance](SUPPORTED-PROFILE.md#governance).
No CPU parallelism, forced production GC, parsed-document cache, native bridge,
dependency/version change or package publication is introduced.
